Privacy Policy — ClipFlow Studio

Official URL: https://georafap.github.io/clipflow-studio/privacy-policy · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Overview: This Privacy Policy describes how ClipFlow Studio (https://georafap.github.io/clipflow-studio/) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our web and desktop application.

2. Data We Access via TikTok API: When you authorize ClipFlow Studio via TikTok Login Kit, we request access only to basic profile metadata (user.info.basic) and content publishing permissions (video.publish, video.upload) required to display your creator profile and upload videos you explicitly submit.

3. Data Storage & Security: OAuth tokens are used strictly to execute user-initiated API requests to open.tiktokapis.com. ClipFlow Studio does not sell, rent, or share personal data or creator credentials with any third party.

4. Revoking Access: You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time within ClipFlow Studio or directly from the TikTok app under Settings and Privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
