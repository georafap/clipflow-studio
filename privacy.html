Privacy Policy — ClipFlow Creator Studio

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Data We Access: When you authorize ClipFlow Creator Studio via TikTok Login Kit, we request access only to basic profile metadata (user.info.basic) and content publishing permissions (video.publish, video.upload) required to display your creator nickname and upload videos you explicitly submit.

2. Data Storage & Security: OAuth tokens are used strictly to execute user-initiated API requests to open.tiktokapis.com. We do not sell, rent, or share personal data or creator credentials with any third party.

3. Revoking Access: You may disconnect your TikTok account at any time within ClipFlow Creator Studio or directly from the TikTok mobile app under Settings and Privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions.
